# Frequently Asked Questions

## What does red mean?

Red means a stronger exposure signal was detected, for example an exposed remote-control port or known remote-control process pattern.

## Can this prove I am being watched?

No. It indicates evidence-based risk and helps prioritize investigation. It does not claim certainty by itself.

## Does this work with App Store submission?

Yes. The repository includes `fastlane` release lanes for building, taking screenshots through deterministic UITests, validating image artifacts, and uploading metadata/screenshot assets with App Store Connect.
